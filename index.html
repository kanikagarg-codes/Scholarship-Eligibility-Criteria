About

Scholarship Eligibility Assistant is a college HTML/CSS/JavaScript project. The dataset is a small demonstration sample – criteria change every year, so use the official links.

FAQ

Is a high match score a guarantee?

No. It only shows how many criteria your entered data satisfies.

Why "Needs Verification"?

Either you left a required field blank, or the scheme is a demonstration entry whose current rules aren't verified.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser and nothing is saved.

Where do I apply?

Use the official link on each card, such as the National Scholarship Portal.
